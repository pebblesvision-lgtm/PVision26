    2. Isolate the stone

    Tap Points around the stone (roughly is fine), drag a Box, or paint the stone with Stone +. Then Run. After that, Stone + and Eraser change the yellow area directly.

    Advanced

    3. Outline

    Move: drag a point. Add point: tap the line where you want one. Delete point: tap the point. Works on straight lines and curves. Re-extracting replaces your edits.

    Advanced

    4. Measure

    Tap two points and type the value you measured. The first value sets the scale.
