1. Photo

Photograph the stone face square-on if you can, with a reference of known size lying flat in the same plane as the face you want to measure: an A4 sheet, a printed marker, or a ruler.

Drop a photo here or click to choose
JPEG / PNG / HEIC, up to 60 MB

Need a marker? Download a 100 mm ArUco marker and print it at 100%. Check the printed size with a ruler before use.

    3. Isolate the stone

    Best on real stone: Points - click along the outer line (roughly is fine), then Run. GrabCut only searches for the exact edge inside the band around your points. Or drag a Box. Fix mistakes with the brushes and the Eraser, undo with Ctrl/Cmd+Z.

    4. Outline

    Detail lines (inside the stone)

    Edit by hand

    Drag vertices, double-click a line to add one, Alt+click to delete. Re-extracting replaces your edits.

    5. Measure & register

    Click two points for a dimension (snaps to outline corners). Then drag the dimension line away from the stone to keep things tidy, like in CAD. Type the tape-measured value to register it; it goes on the drawing with the photo value in brackets.

      6. Finishes & drawing

      Mark hand-tooled finishes as regions: click the corners of the area on the face, then Close region (or double-click). It is hatched with the pattern for that finish and labelled F1, F2… Lines, arrows and leader text for anything else.
